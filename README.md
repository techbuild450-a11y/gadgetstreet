# Gadget Street

**Every shop. One street.**

Gadget Street connects nearby local electronics shops on one platform so customers can compare everything in one place instead of visiting shop after shop.

Built by **Team Tech builders** for International Hackathon 5.0 (Domain 3 – Business Solutions), organized by Kalaignar Karunanidhi Institute of Technology, Coimbatore and ECLearnix EdTech LLC.

## Problem

- People hesitate to trust online platforms for high-value purchases, so they search local shops.
- Local search is slow: price, freebies, stock and EMI must be checked shop by shop.
- City electronics shops have lost business to large e-commerce platforms.

## Features

- Product search with area selector (Salem)
- Price comparison across shops with stock and EMI offers
- Sort by lowest price, nearest shop or biggest discount
- EMI calculator
- Budget tab: ranked best picks for a spending limit
- Repair tab: find nearby service shops
- Call, WhatsApp and map links to contact shops directly
- Tamil + English interface and voice assistant
- Shop-owner entry with verification and a dashboard to update price and stock

## Status

Working prototype with sample Salem shops and prices (not live quotes).

**Next steps:** Supabase realtime sync, shop signup and approval, customer reviews, delivery partner.
